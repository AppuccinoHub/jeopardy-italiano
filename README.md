# Il Tabellone — Jeopardy d’italiano

A classroom Jeopardy game for **Italian 4 Honors** and **AP Italian**. Mid-to-advanced clues, all in Italian: verb conjugations, culture, city and home vocabulary, geography, and holidays.

## Play today

Open this link on the classroom computer or Chromebook attached to the projector:

**https://appuccinohub.github.io/jeopardy-italiano/**

No login. After the first load, it also works offline (the page caches itself).

### How to run a game

1. Choose 2–6 teams and type their names.
2. Optional: turn on a per-clue timer (15 / 30 / 45 / 60 seconds). Sounds stay off unless you turn them on.
3. Click **Inizia la partita**.
4. Click a tile. Read the clue. Click **Mostra risposta**.
5. Mark the team that got it right (+ points) or wrong (− points). If someone is wrong, another team can still try. **Nessuno / Chiudi** closes the tile with no further score change.
6. Two random tiles are a **Puntata doppia** (Daily Double): pick the team that chose the tile, set a wager, then play as usual. Only that team may answer.
7. When you are ready (or when the board is clear), click **Sfida finale**. Teams wager, then you reveal the final clue and score each team.
8. Use the scoreboard **+ / −** buttons anytime to adjust by 100. **Nuova partita** resets scores and shuffles new Daily Doubles; team names stay.

**Chiave del docente** on the start screen prints every clue and answer, including acceptable variants.

Tips: click the fullscreen button for the projector. Press **R** or **Space** to reveal an answer; keys **1–6** award the corresponding team when the answer is showing.

## Categories

Verbi · Cultura · La città · La casa · Geografia · Feste, plus a Final Jeopardy on Italian history (Festa della Repubblica).
